# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

## Additional Cross-functional Roles (Proposed Additions)

To improve clarity, accountability, and cross-functional collaboration, add the following personas. Each includes responsibilities and typical interactions with existing roles.

### UX Designer
- Responsibilities:
  - Lead user research, synthesize findings into insights.
  - Create wireframes, prototypes, and user flows.
  - Advocate for usability and accessibility.
  - Validate designs with users and iterate.
- Interactions:
  - Partners with Product Managers to turn requirements into user-centered designs.
  - Works with Developers on design handoffs and clarifies UI behavior.
  - Participates in backlog refinement and acceptance criteria discussions.
  - Supports QA by defining UI/UX acceptance tests.

### Scrum Master
- Responsibilities:
  - Facilitate Agile ceremonies (standups, planning, retrospectives).
  - Remove process blockers and escalate impediments.
  - Coach the team on Agile practices and help enforce Definition of Done.
  - Track iteration health and team flow metrics.
- Interactions:
  - Works closely with Project Managers to align delivery cadence and remove external blockers.
  - Supports Developers and QA in process adherence and continuous improvement.
  - Keeps Product Manager informed of team capacity and impediments.

### QA Lead
- Responsibilities:
  - Own test strategy, quality gates, and acceptance criteria verification.
  - Coordinate test case creation, manual testing, and automation coverage.
  - Track test results, nonfunctional testing (performance, security), and triage bugs.
- Interactions:
  - Collaborates with Developers to define testable acceptance criteria.
  - Works with Project Manager to schedule QA cycles and releases.
  - Reports readiness to Product Manager and ensures quality signals are visible in CI.

### DevOps Engineer
- Responsibilities:
  - Maintain CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure-as-code, and environment provisioning.
  - Automate deployments and runbook steps for releases.
  - Maintain observability, monitoring, and alerting for deployed services.
- Interactions:
  - Works with Developers to build reliable pipelines and troubleshoot deployments.
  - Partners with QA to provide test environments and support test automation.
  - Coordinates with Project Manager on release windows and rollback plans.

### Security Champion
- Responsibilities:
  - Advocate for security best practices within the delivery team.
  - Perform feature-level threat modelling and basic security reviews.
  - Coordinate with centralized security teams for escalations and audits.
- Interactions:
  - Works with Product Managers and Developers to identify security impacts early.
  - Raises risks in the Risk Register and assists in mitigation planning.
  - Supports QA to include security checks in acceptance criteria.
